Who We Are

Tennessee

TENBJJ is more than gear, it's a declaration. We're Tennessee-rooted, BJJ-driven, and unapologetically both. The oni mask isn't decoration. In Japanese tradition, it wards off evil and protects the worthy. On the mat, that's exactly what you're doing every single roll.

Every piece we drop carries that duality, the discipline and tradition of Brazilian Jiu Jitsu fused with the grit and pride of Tennessee. Whether you're competing, drilling, or repping in the street, you're carrying both.
